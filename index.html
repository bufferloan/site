Why BufferLoan?

More than payment assistance

BufferLoan isn’t a bank. It isn’t another financing company.

It’s a Payment Protection Program designed specifically for automobile dealerships that want to protect customer relationships while promoting healthier lending portfolios. When customers encounter temporary financial hardships, BufferLoan provides eligible assistance before a missed payment becomes a larger problem.

Protect customers

Life happens. Job interruptions. Medical emergencies. Unexpected expenses. BufferLoan gives eligible customers access to temporary 0% payment assistance when they need it most.

Strengthen dealer relationships

Customers remember dealerships that stand beside them after the sale. BufferLoan creates an experience customers talk about—building trust, loyalty, repeat business, and referrals.

Support healthier lender portfolios

Helping customers remain current can contribute to healthier loan portfolios, stronger dealer performance, and more productive relationships with finance companies.

How BufferLoan works

  1. 1

    The dealership enrolls

    Your dealership joins the BufferLoan Payment Protection Program and can offer it to customers.

  2. 2

    The customer applies

    When an unexpected financial hardship happens, the customer contacts BufferLoan and submits an application.

  3. 3

    BufferLoan assists

    When approved, the customer receives their payment on a lender specific debit/credit card allowing them to make their payment at 0%.
